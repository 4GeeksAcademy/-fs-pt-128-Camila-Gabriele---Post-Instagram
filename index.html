The Itsukushima Shrine in Hatsukaichi Prefecture, Japan, is a World Heritage Site famous for its iconic torii gate that appears to float on the sea off Miyajima Island. Dedicated to gods who protect against maritime disasters and war, its architecture, built over the water, blends harmoniously with the natural surroundings.
